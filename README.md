# Kharch 💸

### Where is my salary?

Kharch is a simple personal finance app built around one question:

**Where is my salary going?**

Instead of only tracking money after it disappears, Kharch helps you plan your monthly money flow, turn your planned expenses into actionable monthly moves, and see what remains.

---

## ✨ What Kharch does

Kharch keeps your monthly finances simple:

**Salary → Expenses → Savings → Monthly Moves → Remaining Money**

Set up your recurring expenses once, then use the monthly view to actually follow through.

### 💰 Plan your expenses

Create and organize your monthly expenses by:

- Fixed
- Living
- Savings

You can edit expenses when needed, reorder them, or remove them with confirmation.

Kharch automatically keeps your monthly numbers in sync.

### ✅ Turn expenses into Monthly Moves

Your planned expenses automatically appear in **This Month → Monthly Moves**.

For example:

- Send money to parents
- Pay rent
- Pay EMI
- Add savings
- Pay subscriptions

Check a move when it is completed.

Expense-linked moves are intentionally managed from the Expenses section, so you don't end up maintaining the same information in two places.

Need to track something extra for a particular month?

Use **+ Add task** to create a one-off monthly move.

### 📊 Understand your month

See the important numbers at a glance:

- Salary
- Fixed expenses
- Living expenses
- Savings
- Personal buffer
- Savings rate
- Monthly progress

No complicated accounting required.

### 🛡️ Emergency fund

Set an emergency-fund target and track your progress toward it.

### 📤 Share your month

Generate a clean visual summary of your monthly finances.

You can:

- Download the summary
- Share it directly from supported devices

### 📱 Built for your phone

Kharch is designed mobile-first while still working comfortably on desktop.

It works through the browser on:

- iPhone
- Android
- Desktop

You can also install Kharch to your device's home screen for an app-like experience.

### 🔐 Your financial data stays private

Kharch uses authenticated accounts and private user data.

Your financial information is associated with your account and protected through the application's authentication and database security layers.
